Our purpose

To make getting fit simple, kind and doable, one meal at a time. No guilt and no confusing numbers, just a clear plan and a little help every day.

Why I built it

Fuel started with my fat friend, who I wanted to help become fit. Most food apps felt too complicated or too harsh, so I built one that’s simple, kind and made to keep you going.

It’s for him, and for anyone else who wants to feel stronger and healthier.
